Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Short version

This site doesn't run analytics, doesn't use tracking cookies, and doesn't collect data through forms. If you contact me, that conversation stays private and is used only to reply to you.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts (no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar) are installed.
  • No contact forms — nothing is submitted to or stored on any server owned by me.
  • No third-party advertising or ad networks.

Direct contact (email, Instagram, Bluesky)

If you reach out through the email link, or message me on Instagram or Bluesky, that conversation is visible only to me and stays on the respective platform. I keep messages only as long as needed to reply to you, and I don't share, sell, or forward your information to anyone else.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may log standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) as part of normal server operation. I don't have access to this data myself, and it isn't used by me for tracking or profiling.

External links

Links to Instagram, Bluesky, and GitHub take you to third-party platforms with their own privacy policies. Once you leave this site, their terms apply, not mine.

Changes to this policy

If anything on this site changes in a way that affects data collection — for example, adding analytics or a contact form — this page will be updated to reflect that before the change goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to have a past message deleted, can be sent to muhammadsoheb91@gmail.com.
